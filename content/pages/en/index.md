## About GlobalLockSummary

Most doors already have a functional mechanical lock. Making doors smart is not about electronics, but about knowing what is inside the door and engineering adapters that reliably interface with it.

All data is CC BY 4.0 open and machine-readable via [`/data/catalog.json`](/data/catalog.json) and [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt).
