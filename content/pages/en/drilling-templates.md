# 📐 Global Official Lock Drilling Templates & Dimensions Guide

Professional locksmiths, architectural hardware specifiers, and smart lock retrofit technicians rely heavily on **1:1 scale drilling paper templates**. Applying these templates directly to the door edge and surface ensures accurate center-punching before drilling or chisel routing.

## 1. North American ANSI A156.36 Deadbolt Drilling Protocol (Schlage B60 / Kwikset 660)

<div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; align-items: flex-start; margin: 24px 0; background: var(--color-bg-card, #f8fafc); padding: 18px; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--color-border, #e2e8f0); flex-wrap: wrap;">

    <a href="/en/locks/us-deadbolt.html">

    <p style="font-size: 0.82em; color: var(--color-text-muted, #64748b); margin-top: 6px;">Fig 1: North American standard 54mm (2-1/8") cross bore & 25.4mm (1") edge bore drilling layout</p>
  </div>
  <div style="flex: 1.4; min-width: 300px;">
    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;">Standard Imperial & Metric Dimensions</h3>
    <ul style="line-height: 1.6; font-size: 0.92rem;">
      <li><strong>Cross Bore</strong>: <strong>54mm (2-1/8 inch)</strong> diameter hole. Drill concentric from both door faces to prevent breakout splintering.</li>
      <li><strong>Edge Latch Bore</strong>: <strong>25.4mm (1 inch)</strong> diameter, minimum depth <strong>76mm (3 inches)</strong> to accommodate full deadbolt retraction throw.</li>
      <li><strong>Dual Backset Fold Lines</strong>: Two standard reference positions: <strong>60mm (2-3/8 inch)</strong> for traditional residential doors, and <strong>70mm (2-3/4 inch)</strong> for commercial/exterior entrance doors.</li>
      <li><strong>Faceplate Recess Mortise</strong>: <strong>25.4 × 57mm (1" × 2-1/4")</strong>, routed to depth <strong>3.2mm (1/8")</strong> flush with door stile edge.</li>
      <li><strong>Door Thickness Range</strong>: Standard <strong>35mm ~ 45mm (1-3/8" to 1-3/4")</strong>; thick door kit required for 50mm+ (2"+).</li>
    </ul>
    <div style="margin-top: 14px; padding: 8px 12px; background: #eff6ff; border-left: 3px solid #3b82f6; border-radius: 4px; font-size: 0.82rem; color: #1e40af;">
      <b>Calibration Rule:</b> Before cutting, verify the 100mm (4-inch) test line on the printed sheet with a steel ruler to guarantee 1:1 printer scale (100% scale, disable 'Fit to Page').
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## 2. European DIN 18251 Mortise Pocket & Cylinder Specs (Euro Profile PZ 72/92mm)

<div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; align-items: flex-start; margin: 24px 0; background: var(--color-bg-card, #f8fafc); padding: 18px; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--color-border, #e2e8f0); flex-wrap: wrap;">

      <img src="/assets/img/indigenous/de-gefahrenfunktion.jpg" alt="DIN 18251 Mortise Template" style="width: 100%; border-radius: 6px; object-fit: contain; background: #ffffff; padding: 6px; border: 1px solid #cbd5e1;" />

    <p style="font-size: 0.82em; color: var(--color-text-muted, #64748b); margin-top: 6px;">Fig 2: DIN 18251 / EN 12209 Euro profile mortise dimensions & PZ centers</p>
  </div>
  <div style="flex: 1.4; min-width: 300px;">
    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;">Standard DIN Dimensions</h3>
    <ul style="line-height: 1.6; font-size: 0.92rem;">
      <li><strong>PZ Distance (Center-to-Center)</strong>: <strong>72mm</strong> for interior timber doors; <strong>92mm</strong> for exterior entrance and multipoint PVC-U / aluminium doors.</li>
      <li><strong>Standard Backset (Dornmaß)</strong>: <strong>55mm / 65mm</strong> for wooden rebate doors; <strong>35mm / 45mm</strong> narrow stile for metal profile doors.</li>
      <li><strong>Spindle Square (Vierkant)</strong>: <strong>8 × 8mm</strong> for standard DIN doors; <strong>9 × 9mm</strong> for DIN 18273 fire escape doors (requires <a href="/en/adapters.html#adp-02">ADP-02 adapter</a>).</li>
      <li><strong>Faceplate (Stulp) Dimensions</strong>: 20mm or 24mm width (flat Stulp for wood, U-rail for multipoint frames).</li>
      <li><strong>Cylinder Protrusion Rule</strong>: Must protrude <strong>>= 3mm</strong> from door rose to enable mechanical clamp plate fastening.</li>
    </ul>
    <div style="margin-top: 14px; padding: 8px 12px; background: #eff6ff; border-left: 3px solid #3b82f6; border-radius: 4px; font-size: 0.82rem; color: #1e40af;">
      <b>Emergency Function Requirement:</b> Ensure the Euro cylinder is equipped with double-sided clutch (BS EN 1303 Class 2) so outside manual key override remains operable at all times.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
