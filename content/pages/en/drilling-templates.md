# 📐 Global Official Lock Drilling Templates & Dimensions Guide

Professional locksmiths and smart lock retrofit technicians rely heavily on **1:1 scale drilling paper templates**. Applying these templates directly to the door edge and surface ensures accurate center-punching before drilling.

---

    <h3>Standard Dimensions</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Cross Bore</strong>: <strong>54mm (2-1/8 inch)</strong> diameter. Must be drilled concentric from both sides of the door.</li>
      <li><strong>Edge Bore</strong>: <strong>25.4mm (1 inch)</strong> diameter with a minimum depth of <strong>76mm</strong> to accommodate deadbolt retraction.</li>
      <li><strong>Backset Fold Lines</strong>: Dual reference folds for <strong>60mm (2-3/8") and 70mm (2-3/4")</strong>.</li>
      <li><strong>Faceplate Recess</strong>: <strong>25.4 × 57mm (1" × 2-1/4")</strong>, depth 3.2mm.</li>
    </ul>
